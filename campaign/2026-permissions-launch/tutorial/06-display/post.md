## LinkedIn

Open source and free to use under Apache 2.0, Android ApexFission Permissions helps Android teams show why a feature needs access.

Should a granted permission stay visible? PermissionDisplayMode.All keeps granted pages and green check badges. MissingOnly hides granted pages from the carousel and strip. Both still gate on the original required set and request missing access.
