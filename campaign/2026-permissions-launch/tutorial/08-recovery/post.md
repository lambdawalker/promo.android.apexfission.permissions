Open source and free to use under Apache 2.0, Android ApexFission Permissions helps Android teams show why a feature needs access.

What happens after Android stops showing a permission prompt? The UI can offer app settings when missing access cannot be prompted. Customize recoveryContent, settingsActionLabel, and onOpenSettings. Phrase the state as inferred; the user taps before Settings opens.
